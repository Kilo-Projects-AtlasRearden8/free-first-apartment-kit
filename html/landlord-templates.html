💻 How to use

Fill in the blanks for any template, copy the text, and paste it into an email, text message, or letter. Keep a copy of everything you send (and any reply) — screenshots or a dedicated folder work fine. These are templates, not legal advice.

💻 Maintenance request

When to use: anything broken, leaking, or unsafe — appliances, plumbing, heat, electricity, locks, pests.

💻 Rent payment question

When to use: confirming rent was received, asking about late fees, or requesting a receipt.

💻 Security deposit return request

When to use: after move-out, to request your security deposit back within your state's deadline.

💻 Notice to vacate / lease non-renewal

When to use: telling your landlord you're leaving at the end of your lease. Check your lease for required notice (often 30 or 60 days).

💻 Lock / key request

When to use: lost keys, broken locks, or requesting an extra key.

💻 Noise / neighbor issue

When to use: recurring noise, smoking in shared areas, or other neighbor issues you want the landlord to address.

💻 Move-out inspection request

When to use: scheduling a walk-through before you move out so you can fix anything and protect your deposit.
