# Move-In Inspection

Document the apartment's condition when you move in. Protects your security
deposit.

## How to use

Fill this out within your first few days and go through it with your
landlord if possible. Take photos of anything already damaged, save them,
and keep a copy of this form. Both tenant and landlord can sign at the
bottom.

## Condition key

Mark each item **G** = good, **NR** = needs repair, **D** = damaged. Describe
any damage in the notes column and photograph it.
