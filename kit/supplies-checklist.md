# First Apartment Supplies Checklist

Room-by-room shopping list. Check boxes on screen or print blank and tick
by hand.

## How to use

Check items off as you buy them. Print this page before you shop ("Save as
PDF" works too), or keep it open on your phone. Nothing is saved online — if
you close the tab, your checks are gone, so screenshot or print if you want
to keep it.
